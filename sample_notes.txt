Company Handbook — Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week. All remote employees
must be reachable during core hours, 10am to 3pm in their local timezone.

Equipment: The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 stipend for
home office setup (desk, chair, monitor). Requests go through the IT
portal and are typically approved within 3 business days. 

Expense reimbursement: Internet and phone bills are reimbursed up to $50
per month with a submitted receipt. Reimbursements are processed on the
last Friday of each month.
 
Time off: Employees accrue 15 vacation days per year, plus 10 public
holidays. Unused vacation days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into
the following year. Sick leave is separate and uncapped, subject to
manager approval for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Onboarding: New hires complete a 2-week onboarding program covering
company tools, security training, and a shadowing period with their
team lead. IT accounts are provisioned before the employee's first day.
